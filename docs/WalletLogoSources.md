# Wallet logo provenance

Retrieved and visually checked on 2026-09-21 and 2026-09-22 (per-entry dates are in the manifest). These images identify the wallets named in the app's compatibility notes. Their presence does not imply endorsement, certification, or a verified signing round trip with that wallet. Trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.

All logos are bundled in the asset catalog; the app never downloads them. Every image is the wallet's current **official app icon**, normalized to the same size: **180 × 180 px PNG** (`icon.png` in `wallet_<name>.imageset`). The machine-readable [source manifest](WalletLogoSources.json) records the App Store identifiers, exact source URLs, byte sizes and SHA-256 of both the downloaded and the packaged files.

## Method

- **iOS wallets**: artwork from the Apple iTunes Lookup API (`https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=<id>&country=us`, `artworkUrl512`, requested as a 512 × 512 PNG). Each App Store ID was matched to the developer, bundle ID, and the App Store link published on the wallet's own website. `scripts/fetch-wallet-logo.py` reproduces these entries.
- **Desktop and extension-only wallets** (Sparrow, Specter, Bitcoin Safe, Electrum, Ambire) have no App Store listing. Their icon comes from the wallet's official GitHub repository at a pinned commit, taken from the file the project's own build uses:
  - Sparrow: `sparrow-icon@2x.png` (512 × 512) from `sparrowwallet/sparrow` at `f7f36d00ca13a1ec89bb8b8d85aa6161882707f7`.
  - Specter: the Electron desktop app icon `pyinstaller/electron/icons/icon.png` (1024 × 1024) from `cryptoadvance/specter-desktop` at `5d3d5b91e614991c2dedb87c0e0e608e2c6382ae`.
  - Bitcoin Safe: the packaging icon `tools/resources/icon.png` (1024 × 1024) from `andreasgriffin/bitcoin-safe` at `58b73381ca407865c1058c28afa5a72a440e30ee`.
  - Electrum: the macOS app icon `electrum/gui/icons/electrum.icns` (referenced by `contrib/osx/pyinstaller.spec`) from `spesmilo/electrum` at `0f6318524695f44cdc992a316734bea32688d692`; its largest representation (1024 × 1024) was decoded. The repository's `electrum.png` shows the same mark but is only 128 × 128.
  - Ambire: the app icon `ios/Ambire/Images.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/App-Icon-1024x1024@1x.png` (1024 × 1024) from `AmbireTech/extension` at `00ee620832a5192b34702dc0e605548a1f0df750`.
- Conversion: Pillow LANCZOS downscale to 180 × 180, optimized PNG. App Store artwork is opaque and square; iOS applies its own corner masking, and the app clips the image with a rounded rectangle. Nothing was redrawn, recoloured or generated.

## Notes

- BitTool Wallet: "BitTool Wallet: Crypto Wallet" by BITTOOL PTE. LTD. (the developer of Ownbit), linked from bittool.com's App Store button. Listed for Connect › Wallet Level at the user's request (2026-09-22).
- Specter, Bitcoin Safe and Electrum: like Sparrow, these are desktop icons with a transparent margin (Specter and Bitcoin Safe are macOS-style rounded tiles; Electrum is the bare atom mark with no tile), so they render slightly smaller than App Store artwork inside the app's rounded clip.
- Ambire: no Ambire app is listed on the US App Store and ambire.com offers only the browser extension. The icon is the 1024 × 1024 app icon in Ambire's official `AmbireTech/extension` repository (the extension and mobile codebase); the extension's own manifest icon, `src/web/assets/images/xicon@128.png`, is the same mark on the same purple but only 128 × 128.
- OKX Wallet: the standalone **OKX Wallet** app by OKX Technology Inc, linked from the App Store button on web3.okx.com/download; it is not the OKX exchange app (App Store 1327268470, OKX Inc.).
- BULL Bitcoin: linked from wallet.bullbitcoin.com (the Bull Bitcoin mobile wallet); published by Satoshi Portal Inc., the company behind Bull Bitcoin.
- Bitcoin Keeper: linked from bitcoinkeeper.app; published by Bithyve UK Ltd. The App Store artwork has white corners baked in around a rounded tile.
- OneKey: linked from onekey.so; the App Store artwork has black corners baked in around a rounded tile.
- ZEUS: linked from zeusln.com; published by Atlas 21 Inc.
- Nunchuk: linked from nunchuk.io. Trust Wallet: linked from trustwallet.com. Phantom: linked from phantom.com. Jupiter: linked from jup.ag/mobile (Jupiter Mobile, published by Raccoon Labs Pte. Ltd.). Helium Wallet: linked from wallet.helium.com and docs.helium.com (published by Nova Labs, Inc.).
- TokenPocket: the iOS listing is published by TP Global Ltd; tokenpocket.pro's iOS download page links to this App Store ID.
- Rabby: published by OPCODE LABS PTE. LTD. with bundle ID `com.debank.rabby-mobile`; rabby.io links to this App Store ID.
- Backpack: linked from backpack.app.
- App icons change over time; compare the recorded hashes when reproducing them. Review each brand's guidelines before public distribution; this record is not a trademark license.
